# Understanding of Focus Bear's Mission and Vision

## Task

### Why do you think Focus Bear was created?

I think Focus Bear was created largely to help people - particularly targeting those who have ADHD or experience difficulty focusing in today's modern digital climate. To be more particular, I think it was created because the CEO, Jeremy, found through his own lived experience that though there are tools to help people with ADHD/autism, they were often not actually designed by people with these diagnoses or with a true, scientific understanding of how these diagnoses affect daily habits and concentration thus leading to a feeling of being underserved, or that the tools were not actually properly designed/well-suited to help people with ADHD/autism such as being "too gentle" with the blocking, resulting in distractions still rearing their ugly head and productivity being unchanged.

### What problem is Focus Bear solving?

The problem Focus Bear is solving the issue of there being a lack of affordable, scientifically-backed tools to help people with ADHD and autism increase their productivity, healthily create good habits, and be free from distractions in a manner that is highly customizable to fit your specific needs.

### Why do you think this mission is important?

For me, there are a few reasons I think this mission is important. Firstly, accessibility - as stated in the Focus Bear website, as well as in the article I found for the onboarding task "Working with Vulnerable Populations," the biggest hurdle to accessing help for diagnoses like ADHD or autism is monetary, not social or mental, with many people who struggle with these diagnoses being locked out of support therapy or coaching that they need as it is incredibly expensive. With Focus Bear, they are creating an affordable alternative that will actually allow for people who may not be as financially stable to get access to tools that can help them. Secondly, necessity - even though we have a far greater understanding of ADHD since its first official recongition in 1987, many tools today that are created for people with ADHD, are not actually created with them in mind leading to many focusing/habit-building tools being ineffective for the populations that require it. So, with Focus Bear aiming to create a product more specifically aligned to solving these problems, I think it is important as it will create a solution that will actively help many people.

### How does Focus Bear's work align with your personal values and interests?

I would say Focus Bear's work very much aligns with my personal values and interests. In general terms, I feel aligned as this is a product designed to, at baseline, help people. Growing up, I had always associated innovation and invention with helping people - improving the flow and challenge of our lives so it becomes a bit easier for everyone - so I have always pictured myself working on a product or project that actually improves the quality of people's lives and Focus Bear's work directly aligns with this. Outside of the general reasons, I do have more personal interests towards this that do align, as I have personally seen the effects of ADHD on a person's productivity and mental health, which I will go into later, and being able to work on and help refine a product that will actually improve the quality of a person's productivity and mental health when having ADHD sounded like an incredible opportunity I should not pass up.

### Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?

I do - though I do not particularly struggle with these challenges, many of my friends along with my partner who I live with do as they have ADHD, so I get to witness the struggle, frustration, and anxiety that comes along with it. Frequently during the day there are moments of panic for when an object has magically gone missing despite being used not more than two minutes earlier, or realizing halfway to a destination that we had forgotten something due to being distracted, or (a more common event) required medication are forgotten to be taken requiring a swift return home/hoping that it still works as effectively as planned. When hearing I had been accepted to the role, both of us were elated as it sounds like a fantastic product that we can test out and see if it would help her in her daily struggles, as well as just being able to help out and improve this amazing tool.
