### What AI tools are typically used for your role?

For my role as a bug hunter/usability tester, the main AI tool types that are typically used would be general-purpose reasoning assistants (ChatGPT, ClaudeAI, Gemini, etc.), proxy/Web traffic analyzers (BurpGPT, Caido, etc.), autonomous agents (PentestGPT, Vulnhuntr, etc.), and static code analyzers (Semgrep + AI, Nuclei + AI Workflows, etc.)

### What are the benefits and risks of using AI in a professional setting?

Benefits:
     - Increased efficiency and productivity
     - Enhanced decision-making
     - More available customer/employee support
     - Cost/Time saving
     - Creative/technical assistance

Risks:
     - Errors/hallucinations
     - Data privacy/cybersecurity
     - Bias/ethical concerns
     - Over-reliance/skill erosion
     - Job displacement
     - Plagarism

### What types of information should never be entered into AI tools?

Sensitive, confidential, or private data, such as:
 - Personally identifiable information
    - Ex. Name, DOB, home address
 - Financial details
    - Ex. Credit card numbers, bank account numbers, PINS, transaction records
 - Login credentials/secrets
    - Ex. Usernames/Passwords, API keys, authentication tokens
 - Health Data
    - Ex. medical records, patient data, mental health notes
 - Business/Intellectual property
    - Ex. trade secrets, source code, unpatented inventions
 - Legal/Confidential documents
    - Ex. contracts, client agreements, active litigation documents
 - Other
    - Ex. confidential customer records, employee performance reviews

### How can you fact-check and validate AI-generated content to ensure accuracy?

The easiest method is to always treat AI outputs as unverified text and to locate and compare the output with reliable, verified primary sources. To be specific:
    - Verify all citations used by the AI
        - Bonus: ask for the links ahead of time
    - Cross-reference data and claims
        - Utilize trusted non-partisan, government, or academic repositories for primary sources
        - Consult experts on the subject (if possible or available)
        - Compare the AI output with the trusted primary sources to see how reputable mediums discuss the topic
    - Seek out internal inconsistencies
        - Proof-read the output and search for inconsistenties, look for contradictions, spelling mistakes, etc.
        - Verify the timeliness of its claims (is it using up-to-date info?)
        - Re-run the prompt and see if the answer changes or falls apart
    - For media
        - Examine images for typical AI issues, anatomical distortions, bad perspectives, nonsensical text
        - Reverse image search your images! Ensure they are not stolen
        - For film, ensure that the video is not robotic (unusual verbal pacing, mismatched audio, missing background noise)

### When should you use AI for assistance, and when should you rely on your own skills?

AI should be used for assistance when the tasks are:
    - repetitive or mechanical tasks
    - high-volume data processing (with low-stakes/non-sensitive data)
    - brainstorming
    - learning/troubleshooting errors
    - low-stakes tasks with easy verification

When AI should NOT be used:
    - dealing with sensitive data or information
    - core skill development (avoid AI when trying to improve your skills and practice)
    - high-stakes decisions (rely on human judgement for incredibly important, irreversible decisions)
    - interpersonal interaction (for situations where human trust and nuance are required)
    - when authenticity/evaluation is required

### How can you avoid over-reliance on AI while still benefiting from it?

Over-reliance on AI can be avoided by ensuring that human judgement and learning remain the center of your workflow, where the AI acts as a mental sparring partner or as a medium for mental discussion over replacing your own thinking and reasoning. So ideally, attempt to perform and complete all of the tasks yourself first, when fully struggling or stuck then ask the AI for clarification or pointers, not just ask for answers, and implement the advice given with your own work to reach the best possible outcome for the task at hand, while also learning how to avoid or fix similar issues in the future.

### What steps will you take to ensure data privacy when using AI tools?

When viewing the OAIC guidelines for privacy and the use of commercially available AI products, the steps I will follow are:

1. Ensure that no sensitive information is entered into any AI prompts
    a. Ex. no secret keys, no sensitive employee or customer information, etc.
2. If personal information needs to be entered, anonymize it using fabricated placeholders
3. DO NOT opt in for model training when using any AI models
4. Avoid free tiers of AI tools
5. Ensure that the Focus Bear privacy policy is being followed exactly
6. Regularly clear chat history with AI agents
