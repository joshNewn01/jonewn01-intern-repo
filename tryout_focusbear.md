# Try out the Focus Bear App

## Short Preface

Unfortunately, I have already installed and used the app prior to reaching this onboarding task, but as I had created an account using a personal email as opposed to my school-associated one I did have to create a new account and set everything up again. So, from this, I will catalogue any frustrations, confusions, or areas of unclearness that I ran into while resetting my Focus Bear app.

## Notes of confusion, frustration, or unclearness

### Example 1 

I do not have a screenshot for this, but I personally found the initial download walkthrough to be a bit frustrating, as I did not particularly like having control taken away for a short time when I wanted to dive in and explore the app myself.

### Example 2

For the next point, I found some slight confusion/unclearness with setting the habits themselves, as when I am creating a new habit it gives me the option of nesting additional habits within the overall habit, which is confusing to me as I am unsure how I can effectively use the nested habits – how do they apply? When will they start during the routine?

### Example 4

Though I do not have a screenshot to show this, I have recorded it in the Bug Reports with an attached video – I found that occasionally when rapidly switching between tabs in the Windows version of the app, the tabs sometimes do not load correctly, requiring extra clicks on the tab to get it to open the correct page. Specifically, the first click is ignored, and the previously selected page remains open, instead of loading the selected tab’s page. (Please see bug report 0G9ZYH for more information).

### Example 5

I found some confusion and unclearness in the ‘Notes’ tab specifically with the “Brain Dump” section – upon first look, I thought the Brain Dump section would be a quick way to create a note, as I could type in something quickly and then have it converted to the notes that would be stored below – especially since the option to use the “Brain Dump”. However, I found that this is just a text box to store the idea, and for you to create a note with the text entered into the “Brain Dump” textbox must be completely rewritten to make a saved note out of this, which may cause some issues for wanting to remember some good on-the-spot ideas. I have submitted an idea to the Bug Reports section mentioning how some sort of ‘Quick Add Note’ for the Brain Dump could be implemented (see Bug Report 6UA8PB).
