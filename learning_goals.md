# Set Your Learning Goals

## Reflection

### What do you hope to learn from this internship?

For me, the thing I am hoping to learn the most from this internship is how the process of software development actually works in a real, live environment. Though I have participated in academic simulations of a real software development cycle, none have fully captured all the challenges and learning points of working in a multidisciplinary team working towards the same goal - which I am incredibly keen to experience and work through.

### What skills are you most excited to develop?

For me, I am most excited to develop my testing-related skills, as this has been an area that I have always felt the weakest in and getting an opportunity to get real advice and experience working in a QA/testing team seems like an amazing opportunity to improve my skillset in this area, as well as get a better idea of the hurdles and steps taken during the process of testing and bug-fixing so that I may be able to better plan out future projects as well as be able to ensure that I am using the most efficient and effective ways of testing.

### what do you think you can contribute to the team?

I think there are a few things I can contribute to the team, and the project as a whole. Personality-wise, I will be able to provide a good, positive attitude and a readiness/eagerness to learn. Technicality-wise, I feel that I would be able to contribute quite a bit - hosting guided testing sessions with friends/loved ones to get direct feedback from potential/new users on the application (seeing what areas cause user retention falloff), as well as be able to conduct user testing myself along with constructing automated tests.

### What is one area you feel less confident in that you'd like to improve?

The one area I would like to improve is the previously mentioned QA/testing area of my skillset. Though I am quite confident in the basics of constructing/conducting tests in software, I have always felt it is one of the weaker areas of my skillset and would love to get advice and guidance on how to more effectively and efficiently design automated testing suites, as well as the proper documentation for recording the tests and their results. I am also quite keen to work with and learn how to properly utilize Swift, as it is a language that I have worked with before, but not to the same degree as languages such as Java, JS, HTML or Python.

### What does success look like in this internship to you?

For me, success in this internship would be actually producing real results that actually aid the function of the Focus Bear app (i.e. identifying and/or fixing bugs), as well as not only building confidence in my professional and interpersonal skills, but also developing and improving my industry-based technical skills so that I feel I am ready to fully enter the industry and begin my career in software development.
